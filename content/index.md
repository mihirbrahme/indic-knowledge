# Indic Knowledge

A source-first exploration of ideas from India's textual and intellectual traditions.

Each entry begins with an authentic passage or concept, explains it in context, explores how it has been interpreted, and asks why it may still be interesting today.

## The method

**Discover → Original → Understand → Perspectives → Connect → Explore**

The project distinguishes between:

- what the original text says;
- traditional interpretation;
- historical interpretation;
- modern conceptual parallels;
- modern scientific evidence.

Ancient texts are first understood on their own terms before any modern comparison is made.

## What will appear here

Over time, this site will grow through:

- daily entries;
- verified passages;
- source-text pages;
- concepts;
- thinkers and commentators;
- deep dives;
- learning pathways.

The aim is not to produce inspirational quotations.

It is to make the intellectual depth of these traditions easier to discover.
